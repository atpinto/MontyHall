The reveal
is a clue.

Your first pick has a 1 in 3 chance of winning. The other two doors share a 2 in 3 chance. The host always opens an unchosen door with a goat, so switching to the remaining door wins whenever your first pick was wrong.

Why it isn’t 50–50

The host’s choice is informed, not random: they never reveal the car and always offer a switch. If your first pick is the car (⅓ of games), switching loses. If it is a goat (⅔ of games), the host must reveal the other goat, so switching wins. In this simulation, the host chooses randomly between eligible goat doors when there are two.
